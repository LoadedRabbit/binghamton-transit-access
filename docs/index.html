At night

BC Transit’s last bus out of campus on weekdays leaves at 8:31 pm. After that, only OCCT runs: to the West Side and Downtown until 11:45 pm, but to the Southside and Johnson City’s Main Street only until 9:35 pm. Sunday through Thursday, nothing runs after midnight (OCCT Fall 2026 schedule).

The map

Based on BC Transit’s schedule for Tuesday, October 20, 2026 and Saturday, October 24, 2026.

Pick a view in the box on the map. Each neighborhood is colored by the fastest trip by bus, including the walk to the stop. On Saturdays, about 82% of Broome County’s car-free households can reach a supermarket within 30 minutes, by bus or on foot.

Warning

OCCT isn’t on this map. It shows BC Transit only, since OCCT’s schedules aren’t in BC Transit’s data. Students living near OCCT routes have more options than shown, especially on the West Side.
